# Princess Amira: character bible (AR edition)

Source: the Dabur Amla Arabia short *"👑 أميرة وشعرها القوي والطويل! ✨"* ("Amira and her strong, long hair!")
<https://www.youtube.com/shorts/2FuHfi4WtS0>. These notes come from the short's key frames. **Check them against the series' official style guide before release.**

## Who she is
- **Princess Amira (الأميرة أميرة)** is the hero of the **Dabur Amla Kids** cartoon series.
- She is a young Arab princess, about 6 to 9 years old, drawn in a Pixar-style 3D look.
- Her signature trait is **long, strong, shiny black hair**, which the series credits to Amla Kids hair oil.
- Her personality is cheerful, brave and kind. She doesn't back down (one frame shows a fierce "battle" face against purple dark magic), but most of the time she is playful and spends time with her friends.
- Tagline from the short: **"That's Dabur Amla Kids magic."**

## World and supporting cast seen in the short
- **Beach day:** palm trees, a sea view, a picnic blanket, a fruit basket and a sand castle.
- **Two friends:** one in a blue dress with a blue headband, one with a high ponytail in a black top and pink skirt. Their names aren't in the short. Add them from the series bible.
- **Palace or old-city backdrop** (warm Arabian architecture) for the product moment.
- **Dark/purple magic scene:** a night setting where Amira faces an antagonist.
- **Product hero:** three Amla Kids bottles in **gold, pink and purple**, each with a crown cap.

## Products in the world
The three packs from the range are the magic items in the game. They are built from the official packshots:
- **Nourishing Hair Oil** (gold crown cap): "for longer, stronger & softer hair"
- **Nourishing Detangler** (pink spray): "easy combing for smooth, soft hair"
- **Nourishing Shampoo** (purple crown cap): "gentle cleansing for strong & long hair"

The in-game product lines quote these pack claims word for word. Check them with brand and legal before launch.

## AR story: "Amira and the Tangle Cloud" (proposed)
The **Tangle Cloud (غيمة التشابك)** is a grumpy purple storm cloud that wants to tangle everyone's hair. It's a stand-in for the purple-magic antagonist; swap in the series' real villain if there is one.
1. Amira greets the child and asks for help finding the **three Amla Kids products** hidden around her island. One is above the palace dome, one is in the tall palm and one is over the water.
2. Each bottle flies to Amira, pours its magic into her hair, and her hair **grows longer and shines**.
3. With all three bottles collected, Amira casts **hair magic**, a golden stream that blows the Tangle Cloud away.
4. Everyone cheers and the closing line plays: *"That's Dabur Amla Kids magic 👑✨"*.

Tapping Amira, her friends, the cloud or the beach ball triggers lines, twirls, waves and sparkles. The copy is bilingual (Arabic default, English toggle) in `src/i18n.js`.
